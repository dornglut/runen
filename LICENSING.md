# Licensing

## Current licensing

Runen is available under the GNU General Public License v3.0 only (`GPL-3.0-only`), as provided in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

A separate commercial agreement may be available from copyright holder(s) that have sufficient rights to grant it. Commercial terms are not defined by this repository or by the GPL license text.

Cargo package metadata uses the SPDX identifier `GPL-3.0-only`. A private commercial agreement is a separate contract and is therefore not expressed as an SPDX alternative in Cargo metadata.

## Earlier revisions

This licensing change is prospective. It does not retroactively alter any rights, if any, that applied to earlier revisions. Repository history and the migration issue and pull request preserve the transition evidence.

## Scope and third-party material

The repository license applies only to material to the extent the relevant copyright holder(s) are authorized to license it under these terms. Third-party dependencies, external tools, and separately identified third-party material retain their own license terms. The repository and Cargo license declarations do not relicense those dependencies or external materials.

## Contributions

Commercial dual licensing requires sufficient rights over accepted copyright-bearing repository content. Until Dornglut adopts reviewed inbound contribution terms that preserve the commercial relicensing path, external pull requests that add tracked repository content are not accepted for Runen, including code, documentation, tests, examples, build scripts, or assets.

Issue reports, design discussion, reviews, reproducible cases supplied through discussion, and other participation that does not add third-party repository content may still be accepted under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The governing organization policy is Dornglut Engineering ADR 0007 and the associated licensing standard.
